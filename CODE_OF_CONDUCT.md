# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open and welcoming environment, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive
- accepting feedback gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the project
- showing empathy toward other community members

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, trolling, or insulting language
- personal attacks
- publishing others' private information without permission
- other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a collaborative setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior and may take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to unacceptable behavior.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Reporting

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the project maintainer through the repository hosting platform.
